Important: the current calendar day's value may be incomplete. Do not use it as a finalized full-day metric. Missing dates should not be converted to zero unless XNET defines them that way.

Revenue

Open the human-readable revenue sheet · CSV export

For revenue calculations, use the spreadsheet's GB per month series. The daily offload API is a separate network-usage series and must not be substituted for billing/revenue GB.

WiFi Revenue (Projected)Projected source figure. Not the same as cash received.
WiFi Payment (Received)Separate recorded payment-received series.
Blended Rate per GB (Projected)Projected rate used in the source sheet.

Revenue settlement pipeline

The spreadsheet records different stages of the revenue and Buy & Burn process. These should not be treated as a single same-month metric.

Flow:
Billing GB → Projected WiFi revenue → Payment received → Transfer to Buy & Burn → on-chain BBB / liquidity / burn activity

Historically, WiFi payments have commonly been received roughly two months after the underlying revenue period, although the exact settlement timing varies. A further timing difference can occur before funds are transferred into the Buy & Burn process.

GB per monthRevenue/billing usage basis
WiFi Revenue (Projected)Projected revenue for the source period
WiFi Payment (Received)Cash settlement recorded later
Transferred to Buy & BurnFunds subsequently moved into the BBB process
Transferred to Fiat OperatorsSeparate operator-payment route; preserve independently
Balance Outstanding to TransferSpreadsheet accounting field for remaining transfer balance

Do not assume same-month matching. Projected revenue, received payments and BBB transfers are different stages with settlement timing between them. Blank spreadsheet cells should be treated as null / not reported rather than automatically converted to zero.

Integration rules

Network usageUse the daily offload API.
Revenue calculationsUse the revenue sheet's monthly GB series.
Projected revenueDo not present as realized cash revenue.
Payments receivedKeep separate from projected revenue.
Device countsUse totalDevices and totalOperational as labelled.
Missing observationsDo not zero-fill without a source definition.
